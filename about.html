How the ML Model Works

Our diagnostic pipeline processes plant images through specialized steps to produce a disease assessment with a calibrated confidence score.

1

Leaf Image Capture

A photograph of the plant's leaves is submitted. Image quality directly impacts diagnostic accuracy.

2

Preprocessing & Feature Extraction

The image undergoes color normalization and segmentation. A CNN extracts spatial features — lesion patterns, discoloration areas, and textural anomalies.

3

Classification & Confidence Scoring

Extracted features pass through a binary classifier (Healthy vs Diseased) trained on annotated images. Each prediction carries a calibrated confidence score.

4

Knowledge-Backed Recommendations

Matched detections are cross-referenced with our curated disease database, generating targeted treatment protocols for the identified disease and plant species.

CNN Architecture

Input: RGB image resized to 224×224 px

Layer 1: Conv2d(3→32, 3×3) → ReLU → MaxPool2d(2)

Layer 2: Conv2d(32→64, 3×3) → ReLU → MaxPool2d(2)

Layer 3: Conv2d(64→128, 3×3) → ReLU → AdaptiveAvgPool2d(1)

Classifier: Linear(128→2) → Softmax

Output: [P(Diseased), P(Healthy)]
